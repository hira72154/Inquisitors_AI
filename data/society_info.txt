Complete Society Information Document
1. ABOUT INQUISITORS SOCIETY
                                                                    Inquisitors UET Society is a student-led platform at the University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Lahore. The society is dedicated to nurturing curiosity, critical thinking, intellectual growth, creativity, teamwork, and academic excellence among students.
                        The society organizes quizzes, debates, educational events, workshops, coding sessions, and other knowledge-driven activities that encourage problem-solving, teamwork, creativity, and learning.
Motto:
                 "Fueling Curiosity, Inspiring Intellect."
2. VISION
                    The vision of Inquisitors Society is to create a community of curious learners who excel in:
• Knowledge
• Innovation
• Leadership
• Critical thinking
• Lifelong learning
3. MISSION
                    The mission of Inquisitors Society is to:
                   • Promote critical thinking and curiosity.
                   • Organize educational events and competitions.
                   • Encourage teamwork and creativity.
                   • Prepare students for academic and professional success.
               • Provide opportunities for learning and skill development.
